    <section>

        <p>Hospitality plays a key role in the success of service-based businesses.     <section>
        <h2>Key Principles of Hospitality</h2>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Warm Welcome:</strong> Greet guests with a smile and make them feel at home from the moment they arrive.</li>
            <li><strong>Personalized Service:</strong> Tailor your service to the needs and preferences of each guest.</li>
            <li><strong>Attention to Detail:</strong> Pay attention to the small things that can make a big difference in a guest’s experience.</li>
            <li><strong>Timeliness:</strong> Ensure that guests receive prompt and efficient service.</li>
            <li><strong>Respect:</strong> Treat all guests with respect and make them feel important.</li>
